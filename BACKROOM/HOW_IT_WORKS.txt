HOW IT WORKS - The Backrooms: Level 0
Kobsi · @Kobsi17 · 2026

The whole artwork is one self-contained Python file with no external images or audio files.

1. The world grid
   A procedural floorplan defines infinite-feeling hallways, pillars, and corners.

2. Vectorized raycasting
   Instead of looping pixel by pixel, NumPy marches rays for all screen columns at once
   to find where each column hits a wall, ceiling tile, or floor plane.

3. Fluorescent lighting and surges
   Overhead light panels brighten the ceiling and cast warm reflections onto the carpet.
   During a surge, lights flicker and spike in brightness.

4. Synthesized 60 Hz hum
   pygame.mixer plays a procedurally generated 60 Hz electrical drone with subtle harmonics.

5. ASCII & bloom
   Brightness values map to ASCII character pools, then get layered with downsampled bloom.
