HOW IT WORKS - Birth Flower
Kobsi · @Kobsi17 · 2026

The whole artwork is one self-contained Python file with no external images or audio files.

1. The 366-day almanac
   Special dates and monthly pools assign each day a flower name, meaning, shape archetype,
   and color palette.

2. Parametric 3D particles
   Each archetype uses mathematical formulas for petal curvature, cup depth, and spiral
   arrangements to place thousands of 3D points.

3. Growth choreography
   Particles reveal smoothly from stem to blossom over the opening seconds.

4. Bloom & depth
   3D points are rotated by the camera angle, projected onto the canvas, and layered with
   downsampled additive glow.
