HOW IT WORKS - Jellyfish — Speaking In Light
Kobsi · @Kobsi17 · 2026

The whole artwork is one self-contained Python file with no external images or audio files.

1. Parametric 3D jellyfish
   The bell dome, four lacy oral arms, and twenty-four tentacles are generated as 3D
   point clouds with outward surface normals.

2. Contraction & follow-through
   A smooth swimming cycle squeezes the bell while delayed sine waves ripple down
   the oral arms and tentacles.

3. Nerve pulses & sonar
   Bright cyan and pearl pulses travel along the 8 radial nerve stripes. Right-clicking
   sends a fresh bioluminescent surge through the main bell.
