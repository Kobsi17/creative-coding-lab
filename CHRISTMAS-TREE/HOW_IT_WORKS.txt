HOW IT WORKS - Winter Wonderland
Kobsi · @Kobsi17 · 2026

The whole artwork is one self-contained Python file with no external images or audio files.

1. The snow globe & sky
   Every cell on the ASCII grid checks whether it sits inside the circular glass dome
   or in the night sky outside, where aurora ribbons wave overhead.

2. Layered winter scene
   The pine tree, golden star, crimson ornaments, cabin, and snowman are built from
   vectorized distance checks and revealed progressively.

3. Trail buffer & glow
   Each frame blends into a decaying trail buffer and adds two passes of Gaussian blur
   so the star and ornaments glow warmly.
