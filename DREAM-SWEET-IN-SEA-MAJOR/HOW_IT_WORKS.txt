HOW IT WORKS - Dream Sweet In Sea Major
Kobsi · @Kobsi17 · 2026

The whole artwork is one self-contained Python file with no external images or audio files.

1. The moonlit ocean
   A vectorized shader computes sky gradients, moonlight reflection on the water, and
   animated wave crests.

2. The 3/4 celesta waltz
   Before the first frame, NumPy synthesizes 16 bars of 3/4 waltz chords, celesta
   bell tones, and rolling ocean surf into a stereo pygame sound buffer.

3. Interactive sky & sea
   Dragging shifts the perspective; right-clicking launches a shooting star with a
   chime and ripples a wave across the water.
