CTENOPHORE — HOW IT WORKS

1. Parametric 3D Morphology
   The lobate body is sampled over (u, v) into a flattened biradial bell with
   8 subtle flutes and flaring oral lobes. Eight meridian comb rows (ctenes)
   sit just outside the translucent membrane and carry metachronal cilia waves
   that cycle through cyan, teal, and violet prism bands.

2. Creature Piloting & Trailing Tentacles
   WASD/Arrows and Q/E drive thrust, yaw, pitch, and banking in 3D space under
   viscous underwater drag. Two primary tentacles (68 segments each) and 36
   fine tentilla curtains follow the body in world space with spring-lagged
   motion and sinusoidal water waves.

3. Interactive Plankton & Sonar Ripples
   460 plankton motes live in a wrapping 3D chamber around the creature.
   Left-dragging injects tangential vortex currents and drag wakes; swimming
   through dense plankton boosts the ctenophore's feeding glow. Left-clicking
   spawns an expanding sonar ripple that triggers a prism pulse and chime when
   it reaches the creature.

4. ASCII Projection & Bloom
   Points are depth-sorted onto a 211x78 ASCII cell grid. Silhouette edges
   receive directional outline glyphs (/ \ | - _), interiors use density-ramped
   character pools, and pygame smoothscale + Gaussian blur passes create the
   anisotropic horizontal scanline glow and radial bloom.
