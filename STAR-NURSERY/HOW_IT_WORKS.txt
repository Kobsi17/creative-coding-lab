HOW IT WORKS - Galactic Star Nursery
Kobsi · @Kobsi17 · 2026

The whole artwork is one self-contained Python file with no external images or audio files.

1. Binary nebula & protostars
   Thousands of 3D particles are arranged into nebula clouds and dense star-forming
   regions that ignite after the opening seconds.

2. Fly-through & warp
   Particles stream past the camera in 3D space, wrapping seamlessly as you steer
   with the mouse or toggle warp speed.

3. Lydian soundscape
   NumPy synthesizes a 36-second Lydian drone and harp arpeggios, plus a bright
   chime whenever you trigger a cosmic burst.
