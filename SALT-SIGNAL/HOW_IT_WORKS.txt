HOW IT WORKS - Salt Signal
Kobsi · @Kobsi17 · 2026

The whole artwork is one self-contained Python file with no external images or audio files.

1. Progressive entrance
   Over the first seven seconds, the starry sky and fog fade in, followed by the basalt
   cliff, ocean swells, and lighthouse tower, before the lantern ignites.

2. Fog, waves, and beam
   Layered sine waves form drifting fog and storm breakers. The beam brightness is
   multiplied by the local fog density so it visibly cuts through the mist.

3. Procedural coastal audio
   NumPy synthesizes filtered wind noise, wave swells, a D-minor drone, celesta notes,
   and an interactive 58 Hz nautical foghorn.
