HOW IT WORKS - Spiral Galaxy
Kobsi · @Kobsi17 · 2026

The whole artwork is one self-contained Python file with no external images or audio files.

1. Building the galaxy
   Particles are pre-built in four groups: stationary background stars, diffuse dust,
   four spiral arms of 900 stars each, and a warm central core.

2. Layered reveal & rotation
   Every frame reveals more particles up to the total count while rotating each star
   around the galactic center and projecting the tilted disc onto the screen.
